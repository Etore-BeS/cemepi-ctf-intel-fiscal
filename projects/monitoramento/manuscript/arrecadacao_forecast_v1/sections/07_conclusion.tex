\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The lowest test sMAPE depends on the series and on the grain.
On the month the leader is boosting for the total and for Assessed, and SARIMAX for Declared.
On the week the leader is a linear model, the confidence set stays wide, and the seasonal naive remains inside it.
On the calendar day the forest leads the total and Declared, and the paired test does not reject the seasonal naive at 5 percent.
On Monday to Friday, TimesFM leads all three series, the paired test rejects the seasonal naive, and the confidence set still keeps other models.

The seasonal naive remains the reference on each grain.
A low sMAPE can still miss a peak, as the monthly total does in April.
E1 is not the lowest sMAPE on any cell, and E2 is not the lowest sMAPE.
Publishing one name for every grain would contradict Table~\ref{tab:best}.
