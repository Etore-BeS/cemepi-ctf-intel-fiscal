\subsection{Monthly panel}
\label{sec:monthly}

On the monthly panel the proxy E2 reaches sMAPE $\approx 10.9$ and skill $\approx 0.19$.
Tuned histogram gradient boosting follows at sMAPE $\approx 13.3$ (skill $\approx 0.015$), and the lag-12 seasonal naive follows at sMAPE $\approx 13.5$ (skill zero by~\eqref{eq:ss}).
Selective TEM (E1) is near sMAPE $\approx 15.5$ with negative skill.
It is not the best monthly forecaster in this freeze.

\subsection{Calendar days and business days}
\label{sec:ablation}

Ranking~A scores every civil day, including zeros.
Ranking~B scores Monday--Friday only (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}).
Absolute sMAPE is lower on B, where weekend zeros are absent, and the order changes.
The seasonal naive is second on A and is outside the top three on B, where histogram gradient boosting and ordinary least squares rise behind TimesFM.
TimesFM leads both grains: sMAPE $\approx 59.8$ and skill $\approx 0.20$ on A, and sMAPE $\approx 52.4$ and skill $\approx 0.28$ on B.

The complementary score on days with $y \gt 0$ in Ranking~A is not official.
On those days TimesFM has sMAPE $\approx 29.9$ (skill $\approx 0.58$) and histogram gradient boosting has sMAPE $\approx 34.6$ (skill $\approx 0.51$).
The gap relative to the all-day scores shows how much of Ranking~A is carried by structural zeros.
It is not a reason to drop those zeros from the official target.

\subsection{Weekly aggregate}
\label{sec:weekly}

On the Sunday-close weekly series, TimesFM has sMAPE $\approx 17.2$ and skill $\approx 0.41$ against the lag-52 seasonal naive.
Ridge and ordinary least squares are next.
Most other families have negative skill against that seasonal naive in this freeze.
The weekly test block contains 13 points, so the level of skill is descriptive of this holdout, not a precise estimate of a long-run advantage.
