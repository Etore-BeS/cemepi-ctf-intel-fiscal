\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:best} is the lowest test sMAPE on each series and grain.
The skill score uses the seasonal naive of that grain.
The HLN column is the two-sided p-value of the best model against that naive.
A p-value below 0.05 is marked in the text below, not in the table.
The model confidence set uses level 0.10.

The best model changes with the series on three grains.
On the month, boosting leads the total and Autuado, and SARIMAX leads Declarado.
On the week, ridge leads the total and Declarado, and least squares leads Autuado.
On daily A, the forest leads the total and Declarado, and TimesFM leads Autuado.
On daily B, TimesFM leads all three series.

The HLN test rejects equal accuracy versus the seasonal naive on every daily B series, on the total month, and on the Declarado month.
It does not reject on any weekly series, on total daily A, or on Autuado daily A.
The Declarado daily A p-value is 0.069.
The Autuado month p-value is 0.077.

The 90 percent model confidence set is wide on the month and on the week, where the test block has 12 or 13 points.
On total daily A the set is the forest and two seasonal naives (lag 7 and lag 365).
On Declarado daily A the set contains only the forest.
On daily B the lag-5 naive is outside the set for the total and for Declarado.
TimesFM remains in a set with other models on daily B.
E1 has no row.
The TEM vendor tree is not in this worktree.
