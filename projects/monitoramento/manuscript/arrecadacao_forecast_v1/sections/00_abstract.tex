We forecast how much cash will be collected on enrolled tax debt (Portuguese: dívida inscrita), meaning tax debt formally registered for collection.
The data are one static extract from March 2026.
A grain is the time step of a series.
We use four grains: a monthly panel of debt stock and collection, calendar-daily totals of GARE payment slips (grain A), the same daily slips restricted to Monday--Friday (grain B), and a weekly sum.
GARE denotes the payment-slip aggregate in this extract.
Grain A keeps weekend and empty-slip zeros.
Grain B drops weekends and keeps Brazilian national holidays.
We fit fourteen model families.
Hyperparameters minimize validation sMAPE, the symmetric mean absolute percentage error.
The official rank is test sMAPE.
Lower is better.
A skill score compares each model with the seasonal naive forecast of the same grain.
It equals one minus the ratio of the two sMAPE values.
The best model depends on the grain.
On the monthly panel, the disagreement proxy E2 has the lowest test sMAPE, about 10.9.
E2 switches between gradient boosting and the seasonal naive.
It is not the Time-Energy Model (TEM).
On grains A and B, and on the weekly series, TimesFM 3.0 has the lowest test sMAPE.
Selective TEM (E1) can abstain.
Abstention means returning the seasonal naive when the energy of every candidate is above a validation threshold.
E1 does not have the lowest monthly test sMAPE in this extract.
