VAT (ICMS) is the state value-added tax in this study.
We forecast three collection series from one legal-entity book: the total, Declared (Declarado), and Assessed (Autuado).
Declared is self-reported VAT.
Assessed is VAT from a tax assessment.
In the December stock, Assessed is 72.74 percent and Declared is 27.21 percent.
In collection from 2016 through 2025, Declared is 64.63 percent and Assessed is 35.24 percent.
Each series has four grains: month, calendar day (grain A), Monday to Friday (grain B), and week.
The protocol is the closed v1 benchmark.
One series has one train block, one validation block, and one test block.
The rank is test sMAPE.
The best model is not the same on every series or every grain.
On the month, boosting leads the total and Assessed, and SARIMAX leads Declared.
On Monday to Friday, TimesFM leads all three series.
A skill score, a Diebold-Mariano test with a small-sample correction, and a model confidence set sit beside that rank.
They do not replace it.
The Monday to Friday lead over the seasonal naive is the comparison those tests agree on.
The monthly and weekly test blocks do not support one winner.
