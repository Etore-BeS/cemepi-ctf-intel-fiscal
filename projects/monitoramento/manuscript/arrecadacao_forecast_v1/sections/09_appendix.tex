\section{Calendar-day holdout}
\label{app:pred-a}

Grain A keeps every calendar day, including weekends with many zeros.
Figure~\ref{fig:pred-a} uses the same holdout design as the main text.
Figure~\ref{fig:scatter-a} is the same test block as a scatter.
Figure~\ref{fig:residual-a} is the leader residual.
Test sMAPE on this grain is higher than on grain B for the total and for Declared.
The forest leads those two series.
TimesFM leads Assessed.

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_pred_daily.pdf}
  \caption{Grain A, calendar days.
  The forest leads the total and Declared.
  TimesFM leads Assessed, with the forest within 5 percent, so both lines are drawn there.
  Weekend zeros sit on the axis.
  The vertical axis is millions of BRL.}
  \label{fig:pred-a}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_scatter_daily.pdf}
  \caption{Grain A test block as a scatter.
  A mass of points at the origin is the weekend and empty-slip zeros.
  The large days fall off the diagonal.
  That cloud is why all-day sMAPE stays high even when the leader beats the lag-7 naive on the point rank.}
  \label{fig:scatter-a}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_residual_daily.pdf}
  \caption{Grain A residual, forecast minus actual, for the sMAPE leader.
  Spikes away from zero are the days the leader misses.
  The zeros pull many residuals toward a small value and do not remove the peak misses.}
  \label{fig:residual-a}
\end{figure}

\section{Family-level bar charts}
\label{app:bars}

The main text ranks models with tables.
Figures~\ref{fig:smape-total}--\ref{fig:abl-skill} repeat those ranks as bars.
Teal is the lowest test sMAPE in that panel.
Other bars share one blue, because the model name is already the category label.
A missing bar means that naive is not a candidate on that grain.
Each panel has its own horizontal scale.
Read the printed number when you compare panels.
Bar length is comparable inside a panel, from a zero baseline.

\section{Full model zoo}
\label{app:small-multiples}

Figures~\ref{fig:zoo-total-monthly}--\ref{fig:zoo-autuado-daily} show every saved forecast against realized collection.
Gray is the actual in every panel.
The forecast uses that model's color, the same color as in the main text.
The teal frame is the lowest test sMAPE.
The vertical scale is shared inside a figure, so a spike that leaves the gray line is a large level error.
These panels replace a single overcrowded line plot.
