\section{Calendar-day holdout}
\label{app:pred-a}

Grain A keeps every calendar day, including weekends with many zeros.
Figure~\ref{fig:pred-a} shows the same style of holdout plot as in the main text.
Test sMAPE on this grain is higher than on grain B for every family in Figure~\ref{fig:smape_grain}.
TimesFM 3.0 still has the lowest test sMAPE among the models in the freeze.
