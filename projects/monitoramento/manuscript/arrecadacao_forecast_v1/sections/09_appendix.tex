\section{Calendar-day holdout}
\label{app:calendar}

Figure~\ref{fig:pred-a} is the grain A holdout.
Grain A keeps every calendar day, including weekend and empty-slip zeros.
The lowest test sMAPE on this grain is TimesFM 3.0.
The main text plots the same comparison for the monthly grain, for grain B, and for the weekly series (Figures~\ref{fig:pred-m}, \ref{fig:pred-b}, and~\ref{fig:pred-w}).
