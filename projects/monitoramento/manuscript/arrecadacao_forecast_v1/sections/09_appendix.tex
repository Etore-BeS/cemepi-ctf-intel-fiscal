\section{Calendar-day holdout}
\label{app:pred-a}

Grain A keeps every calendar day, including weekends with many zeros.
Figure~\ref{fig:pred-a} shows the same clean holdout style as in the main text (Actual + sMAPE leader).
Test sMAPE on this grain is higher than on grain B for every family in Table~\ref{tab:top3}.
TimesFM 3.0 still has the lowest test sMAPE among the models in the freeze.
