\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

VAT (ICMS) is a state value-added tax.
This paper forecasts VAT collection, not the mix of every enrolled tax.
The legal-entity book has more than one VAT type.
Declared is self-reported VAT and assessed is VAT from a tax assessment, so a model that fits the total can hide a miss on one of them.
Assessed is most of the stock and declared is most of the cash collected (Section~\ref{sec:data}).

The cash series is seasonal.
At a daily step it is also full of zeros.
A zero is a weekend, a holiday, or a day with no payment slip.
Those zeros are part of grain A.
They are not missing values to drop before scoring.

The four grains are the month, the calendar day, Monday to Friday, and the week.
The rank is test sMAPE.
A skill score, a corrected Diebold-Mariano test, a Wilcoxon signed-rank test, and a model confidence set sit beside that rank and do not replace it~\citep{diebold1995comparing,harvey1997testing,wilcoxon1945individual,hansen2011mcs}.
The pairwise signed-rank counts are in Table~\ref{tab:wilcoxon-pairs}.

\paragraph{Questions.}
Which model has the lowest test sMAPE on each series and each grain?
Does that model change between the total, Declared, and Assessed?
Does the test block separate that model from the seasonal naive, and, pair by pair, from the other candidates?

\paragraph{What we report.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item One protocol for three series and four grains.
        Models are chosen by validation sMAPE and ranked by test sMAPE.
        The skill score, the corrected Diebold-Mariano test, the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, and the model confidence set are further columns.
        They are not the rank.
  \item The lowest test sMAPE belongs to different models on different series and on different grains.
        Keeping calendar zeros or dropping weekends changes both the sMAPE level and the order of models.
  \item Selective TEM (E1) and the disagreement switch (E2) are defined in Section~\ref{sec:energy}.
        E1 has a validation sMAPE and a test sMAPE on every series and grain.
\end{itemize}
