\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Enrolled debt (dívida inscrita) is tax debt that has been formally registered for collection.
The stock of that debt is large and changes slowly.
The cash actually collected is seasonal.
At a daily step it is also full of zeros.
A zero means a weekend, a holiday, or a day with no payment slip.
Those zeros are part of the series.
They are not missing values to drop before scoring.

A grain is the time step we forecast.
This paper uses four grains from one extract: month, calendar day, business day, and week.
GARE is the payment-slip aggregate that supplies the daily and weekly totals.
Grain A is the calendar-daily GARE series, zeros included.
Grain B is the Monday--Friday slice of the same slips.
Grain B is a different target.
It is not a filter applied after scoring grain A.

sMAPE is the symmetric mean absolute percentage error.
We define it in Section~\ref{sec:metrics}.
Lower sMAPE is better.
The official rank on every grain is test sMAPE.
The skill score compares a model with the seasonal naive forecast of that same grain.
We define it in Section~\ref{sec:ss}.
A positive skill score means lower sMAPE than that naive forecast.

TEM is the Time-Energy Model~\citep{brusokas2025tem}.
It scores candidate forecasts with an energy.
Abstention means refusing the energy choice and returning the seasonal naive instead.
We use abstention only for selective TEM, labeled E1.
E2 is a separate rule.
E2 switches between gradient boosting and the seasonal naive when those two forecasts disagree.
E2 does not estimate an energy.

\paragraph{Questions.}
Which model has the lowest test sMAPE on each grain?
On the monthly panel, does selective TEM (E1) have lower test sMAPE than the seasonal naive, and does the disagreement proxy (E2)?

\paragraph{What we report.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item One protocol for all four grains.
        Models are chosen by validation sMAPE and ranked by test sMAPE.
        The skill score is a second column, not the rank.
  \item Separate labels for E1 and E2.
        E1 abstains by an energy threshold.
        E2 abstains by absolute disagreement.
  \item On the March 2026 extract, the lowest test sMAPE belongs to different models on different grains.
        Keeping calendar zeros (A) or dropping weekends (B) changes both the sMAPE level and the order of models.
\end{itemize}
