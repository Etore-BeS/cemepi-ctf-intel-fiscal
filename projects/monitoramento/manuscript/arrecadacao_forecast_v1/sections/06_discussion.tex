\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Ranks do not transfer across grains or series}
\label{sec:grain-dependence}

Table~\ref{tab:top3} does not support one best model.
The monthly panel is short, seasonal, and paired with a stock lag.
There, the leader depends on the series.
Boosting has the lowest test sMAPE on the total and on Autuado.
SARIMAX has the lowest test sMAPE on Declarado.
E2, which switches between boosting and the seasonal naive, does not win that comparison on these VAT series.
The switch is still in the candidate list.
It is not the lowest sMAPE.

The Monday to Friday series are longer.
TimesFM has the lowest test sMAPE on all three, and the paired test rejects the lag-5 naive on all three.
TimesFM does not have the lowest test sMAPE on the month, on the week, or on calendar-day total and Declarado.
The same weights can lead one grain and lose to a seasonal naive on another.
Publishing one name for every grain would contradict Table~\ref{tab:best}.

Monthly and weekly sMAPE in Table~\ref{tab:best} lie well below calendar-day sMAPE.
A month or a week adds up many zero days, so the percentage error falls.
Skill scores tell a different comparison.
A model can have a moderate sMAPE and still sit close to the seasonal naive.
That is the weekly pattern: skill near 0.14 and a p-value above 0.2.
Family-level bars are in Appendix~\ref{app:bars}.

\subsection{Zeros and the lag used in the skill score}
\label{sec:zeros}

On grain A, a copy of last week matches weekend zeros.
A model that smooths through those zeros pays for it in all-day sMAPE.
On the total, the lag-7 naive, the lag-365 naive, and TimesFM are packed between 105 and 107, and the forest leads at 90.52 without a significant paired test.
Scoring only days with a slip would answer a different question.
Ranking A is the error on the calendar, zeros included.

Grain B drops weekends and keeps national holidays (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}).
Test sMAPE falls on the total and on Declarado, and TimesFM leads with a paired test below 0.001.
Skill on B uses lag 5.
Those scores do not transfer to grain A, where the reference is lag 7.
Weekday holidays remain in the series.
No model, including Prophet, receives a holiday indicator.

Autuado stays the harder series on both daily grains.
Its Monday to Friday sMAPE is 90.32, against 65.05 on the total.
The stock share in Figure~\ref{fig:shares} is not a forecast, and it does line up with this gap.
Autuado is most of the enrolled balance and a smaller, more irregular part of the cash.

\subsection{What the leading rules return on the holdout}
\label{sec:rules}

The seasonal naive is the reference forecast on every grain.
Its skill score is zero by construction.
On several grains it is inside the top three by test sMAPE.
A replacement has to beat it on the series and the grain being published.

E1 would return the lowest-energy candidate, or the seasonal naive if that energy is at least the validation threshold.
This run has no E1 score.
The tables cannot say how often E1 would have abstained.

E2 returns boosting, or the seasonal naive when those two forecasts disagree by more than a validation threshold.
E2 does not have the lowest test sMAPE on any cell in Table~\ref{tab:best}.
On the monthly total it is behind boosting used alone.
The disagreement switch does not improve on boosting for these VAT series.
A sentence about E2 is not a sentence about E1.

Figures~\ref{fig:pred-m}, \ref{fig:pred-b}, and \ref{fig:pred-w} plot the test block.
The solid line is realized collection.
A dashed line is a forecast.
Color is the model, and the same color is used in every figure in this paper.
The second dashed line appears only when that model's test sMAPE is within 5 percent of the leader.
The calendar-day holdout is in Appendix~\ref{app:pred-a}.

\subsection{Forecast against actual}
\label{sec:scatter}

A time plot shows when the miss happens.
A scatter shows whether the forecast rises with the actual.
The dashed line is the 45-degree line.
A point on that line is a hit.
A point below it is an under-forecast.
Color is again the model, and the panel title is the series.

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/fig_scatter_monthly.pdf}
  \caption{Monthly test block as a scatter.
  The total does not follow the diagonal: the April point is far to the right and low, so the low sMAPE is a statement about level, not about month order.
  Declarado stays closer to the line, with one high month above it.
  On Autuado the far-right point is the April spike, forecast too low.}
