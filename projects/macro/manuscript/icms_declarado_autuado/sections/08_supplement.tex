\section*{Supplementary Materials}\label{sec:supplement}

The tables and figures in this paper report the descriptive comparison and the fitted models.
Section~\ref{sec:inference} states the linearity and group-heterogeneity diagnostic.
Section~\ref{sec:fitted} reports the specification that diagnostic selects.
A notebook is the supplementary material.
It fits the origin logit, the within-firm linear probability model and the paid-to-balance percentage difference, and it rebuilds the descriptive aggregates.
It does not contain taxpayer identifiers.
The files behind the tables already leave blank any activity cell with one to four firms.
The underlying certificate records are confidential tax data of PGE-SP and cannot be shared.
